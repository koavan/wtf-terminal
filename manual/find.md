# Find

{{cmd:find}} opens a search bar over the focused pane. Type to highlight every match in the scrollback.

- {{cmd:find.next}} and {{cmd:find.prev}} move between matches ({{mac:or ↩ and ⇧↩ in the search field|other:Enter and Shift+Enter in the search field}}).
- **Aa** beside the field makes the search case-sensitive.
- `Esc` closes the bar and puts you back in the terminal.
